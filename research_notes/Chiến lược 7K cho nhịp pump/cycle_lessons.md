## What veteran traders and research consistently say about the early bull (sequencing, relative strength, unlocks, profit-taking, leverage)

### Takeaway
Sourced "veteran" material is thinner and more anecdotal than the price data, but it converges on: (1) BTC leads and alts follow in waves, with broad alt outperformance historically arriving only after BTC reclaims/clears its prior high and dominance breaks lower; (2) most alts lose to BTC over a full cycle, so alt exposure is a timing/selection bet, not a default; (3) supply overhang (low-float/high-FDV, team unlocks) is a measurable headwind; (4) leverage in crypto is structurally fragile because crashes are amplified by liquidation engines, ADL and thin weekend/overnight liquidity.

### Cited Findings
- **Sequencing / wave pattern (Arthur Hayes, written ~early 2023 with BTC ≈ $23K):** the reserve assets (BTC, ETH) rally first; the rally stalls and prices slip slightly while the "shitcoin complex" stages an aggressive rally; then alts "rediscover gravity" and interest shifts back to BTC/ETH; this stair-step repeats until the secular bull ends [quoted, secondary write-up of his essay] — [The Informer Post summary of Hayes](https://theinformerpost.beehiiv.com/p/bitmex-founder-arthur-hayes-says-altcoins-could-go-vertical-in-next-bull-frenzy-heres-his-outlook); [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/markets/altcoins-are-sickly-but-its-time-to-buy-says-arthur-hayes/)
- **Hayes after the 2025-10-10 crash (19 Dec 2025):** altcoins are "sickly"; "it will take time for the altcoin complex to heal"; those who preserved capital should "dumpster dive"; hedge-fund LP redemptions after October forced price-insensitive selling of alts; he expects leverage demand to return first via BTC and then spill into alts [quoted] — [DL News, 2025-12-19](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/markets/altcoins-are-sickly-but-its-time-to-buy-says-arthur-hayes/)
- **K33 Research (June 2023):** $1 put into each of 1,009 altcoins as they entered CMC's top-100 since 2015 would be worth ≈ $7,000 vs ≈ $50,000 for the same dollars in BTC; over two-thirds of those alts became inactive; only 9.11% had positive returns and ≈1.5% beat BTC's ≈50x; altcoin investing was profitable only in 2017 and 2021; alt rallies began only after BTC broke its prior ATH (2017 alt run started after BTC passed its 2013 peak in April 2017); in prior alt seasons BTC dominance fell below 60% and the dominance low coincided with the alt-market top [quoted] — [Cointelegraph on K33, 2023-06-22](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/bitcoin-only-buy-and-hold-investment-strategy-outperforms-altcoins-over-the-long-term-analysis-shows)
- K33's Anders Helseth: "You can create higher returns by trading market sentiment more aggressively," but it "requires a lot of attention and is more risky"; alt portfolios require "timing the market or picking the altcoin winners" [quoted] — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/bitcoin-only-buy-and-hold-investment-strategy-outperforms-altcoins-over-the-long-term-analysis-shows)
- K33 on 2018–19: altcoins "continue[d] to hover around the lows for most of the duration of the bear market and only revers[ed] their trend after Bitcoin broke above its previous peak of $20,000" (i.e., late 2020) [quoted] — [K33 via search summary of Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/bitcoin-only-buy-and-hold-investment-strategy-outperforms-altcoins-over-the-long-term-analysis-shows)
- **Altseason definition used by traders:** Blockchain Center's Altcoin Season Index calls "altcoin season" when ≥75% of the top-50 coins (ex-stablecoins/asset-backed tokens) outperform BTC over a rolling 90 days; ≤25 = "Bitcoin season"; it is backward-looking [quoted] — [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/heres-why-alt-season-may-be-on-the-horizon/); [CoinGate](https://coingate.com/it/gift-cards/articles/article/altcoin-season-what-it-is-and-how-to-spot-one)
- **Relative-strength-on-pullbacks practice:** CryptoCred (secondary report): in past cycles BTC strength fed alts because crypto-native buyers roll profits into other coins, which "led to altcoin front running during Bitcoin pullbacks and consolidations" [quoted, secondary] — [Coinfomania](https://www.coinfomania.com/cryptocred-analyzes-bitcoin-trends-what-this-means-for-altcoins/). Morpheus Trading (Deron Wagner) describes building "a relative strength watchlist of emerging altcoin leadership" (coins near new ATHs while BTC is still below its high) and buying "valid pullbacks or consolidation patterns" [quoted] — [Morpheus Trading](https://morpheustrading.com/blog/4-leading-cryptos/)
- **Token unlocks (Keyrock, 16,000+ unlock events):** ~90% of unlocks push prices down regardless of size/type; price impact often starts ~30 days before the unlock; larger unlocks bring 2.4x sharper drops; team unlocks are the worst (≈ −25%), investor unlocks more controlled, ecosystem unlocks slightly positive (+1.18% avg) [quoted] — [Keyrock](https://keyrock.com/from-locked-to-liquidity-what-16000-token-unlocks-teach-us/); [crypto.news summary](https://crypto.news/token-unlocks-almost-always-negative-for-price-keyrocks-study-reveals/). Caveat: The Tie's study of 12,350 priced unlocks found the median unlock small relative to daily volume — [Streamflow](https://streamflow.finance/blog/token-unlock-report)
- **Low float / high FDV (Binance Research, May 2024):** ≈ $155B of tokens scheduled to unlock 2024–2030; tokens launched in 2024 had an average MC/FDV of just 12.3%, implying ≈ $80B of new demand needed just to hold prices [quoted] — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/binance-urges-small-medium-projects-high-fdv-trend); [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/binance-doubles-down-on-low-float-high-fdv-calls-for-action/); [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/binance-highlights-155-billion-token-unlock-overhang/)
- **Leverage and exchange plumbing:** On 2025-10-10, Binance paid $283M compensation for collateral (USDe/wBETH/BNSOL) mis-marked during 21:36–22:16 UTC; USDe printed $0.65 on Binance while ~$0.99 on Curve/Uniswap — i.e., liquidations were triggered by venue-specific prices [quoted] — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/newsletter/defi-daily/the-ultimate-10-10-crash-autopsy); Hayes says he personally uses little or no leverage and finds shorting hard [quoted, secondary] — [search summary of Hayes interviews](https://ceointerviews.ai/interview/1076739/)

### Inferences
- The best-documented sequencing rule is K33's: broad alt outperformance arrived only after BTC cleared its prior ATH (2017, late 2020–21). In 2019 and 2023 (BTC still far below its prior ATH) breadth was poor except for narrative leaders — consistent with my computed scoreboard. The 2026 setup (BTC ≈ $81.5K vs $126.2K ATH) is the "below prior ATH" regime, which historically favoured a few leaders, not baskets.
- Unlock data argue for checking each watchlist coin's next-90-day unlock schedule and MC/FDV before sizing; team unlocks are the most damaging category.
