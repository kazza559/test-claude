## Q1. What does Saturn issue, what backs it, where does the yield come from, and what is the current and historical APY?

### Takeaway
Saturn (the "Saturn Foundation", X handle @saturn_credit) issues two tokens:
- **USDat**, a permissioned-mint stablecoin. It was backed 1:1 by M0's `$M` (tokenized T-bills) until Aug 19, 2026. Since then it has been backed by **PYUSDx**, a wrapper of PayPal's PYUSD (issued by Paxos). Since about Sep 9, 2026 it pays a 3% APR.
- **sUSDat**, an ERC-4626 vault whose assets are 100% Strategy's **STRC** perpetual preferred stock, held off-chain. Its NAV is marked to STRC's market price.

The yield is STRC's dividend, currently 12.00% a year. sUSDat is not price-stable. Its USDat exchange rate fell about 15.7% during the June 2026 STRC crash, and from Apr 1 to Sep 28, 2026 it gained only about 3.05% in total (about 6.3% annualized). Headline "APY" figures of 13–16% (DefiLlama, Pendle) are inflated by STRC's price recovering toward par.

**sUSDat mechanics**
- sUSDat is ERC-4626 with 18 decimals; the underlying USDat has 6 decimals. Other parameters:
  - 10 bps deposit fee.
  - Rewards vest linearly over 30 days.
  - Minimum withdrawal is 10 USDat.
  - The share price formula is `totalAssets = usdatBalance + (vestedStrcBalance × strcPrice)`. It is marked to market on STRC's price.
  - — [sUSDat Overview](https://saturncredit.gitbook.io/saturn-docs/solution/susdat-overview); [Staking & Unstaking](https://saturncredit.gitbook.io/saturn-docs/solution/susdat-overview/staking-and-unstaking-process)
- Unstaking works in three steps: request (the user receives an NFT receipt), processing, and claim. In processing, "Saturn's processor locks a batch of requests and sells the corresponding STRC on secondary markets to obtain USDat". The docs expect this to take "approximately 3–7 days", say it works "similarly to a limit order", and say it "is expected to improve materially following Saturn's V2 upgrade". — [Staking & Unstaking](https://saturncredit.gitbook.io/saturn-docs/solution/susdat-overview/staking-and-unstaking-process); [Orbital Points – Season 2](https://saturncredit.gitbook.io/saturn-docs/overview/orbital-points-season-2)
- Fees: "Saturn charges a 10% protocol fee on the yield generated by sUSDat. To support early adoption, 100% of this fee will be returned to sUSDat holders during an initial bootstrap period." A risk reserve is funded at launch by the $M yield from USDat reserves. — [Fees and Risk Reserve](https://saturncredit.gitbook.io/saturn-docs/operations-and-governance/fees-and-risk-reserve)
- STRC custody: STRC is "held offchain through a BVI professional fund structure". It is "custodied with Clear Street, Galaxy's custodian partner", and the fund is "administered by Securitize". — [Custody of Digital Credit](https://saturncredit.gitbook.io/saturn-docs/operations-and-governance/custody-of-digital-credit)
- Upcoming change: Ondo's tokenized STRC ("STRCon") will be integrated into sUSDat. The Aug 13–14, 2026 announcement did not give implementation timing or say whether STRCon replaces directly held STRC. — [CryptoTimes, Aug 14, 2026](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/08/14/saturn-partners-with-ondo-to-bring-tokenized-strc-into-defi/); [KuCoin blog](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/saturn-partners-with-ondo-to-bring-institutional-tokenized-assets-into-strc-structured-products)

**Yield source: STRC (Strategy "Stretch")**
- STRC is Strategy's variable-rate perpetual preferred stock, with $100 stated value, paid monthly (now semi-monthly), and cumulative. The rate is adjusted monthly to keep the price near par:
  - monthly VWAP below $95: +50 bps or more;
  - $95–98.99: +25 bps or more;
  - $99–100.99: ±25 bps at management's discretion;
  - $101 or above: −25 bps or more.
  - — [Saturn docs: Digital Credit Strategy](https://saturncredit.gitbook.io/saturn-docs/solution/susdat-overview/digital-credit-strategy)

### Inferences
- sUSDat is best understood as a **mark-to-market, liquidity-transformed claim on one Nasdaq preferred stock (STRC)**, not a "stablecoin". Holder return is roughly the STRC dividend (12% today) plus or minus STRC price moves, minus fees and slippage. With STRC at $98.54 on Sep 25, there is roughly 1.5% of upside left if STRC returns to $100 par. After that, forward yield should converge toward the 12% dividend (the 10% protocol fee is currently rebated).
- The 13.6–16.3% "APY" readings in September 2026 are backward-looking and inflated by STRC's recovery from about $85 to $98.5 (July to September). They should not be extrapolated.
- The exchange rate was almost flat between Sep 15 and Sep 28 (1.030368 to 1.030483). A likely contributor is the 30-day linear vesting of dividends together with STRC trading flat at about $98.5. This is an inference and I have not verified it against vesting events.

### Gaps
- I found no official Saturn page that states the funding source and mechanics of the new USDat 3% APR, or how long it will last. The only sources are the Coinfomania/@pendle_fi report and Pendle's API reward field.
- I could not verify the exact current fee status (whether the 10% sUSDat protocol fee is still 100% rebated) or the timing of the V2 upgrade and STRCon integration.
- I did not reproduce the sUSDat exchange rate on BNB Chain or Monad. Those vaults may differ slightly.

## Q2. Chains, launch dates, and total supply / TVL now and over time

### Takeaway
- **Launch:** contracts were deployed Mar 10, 2026. A private beta followed (about $10M from Flowdesk, Galaxy and others). Public mainnet and Gravity Points began Apr 8, 2026.
- **Chains:** Ethereum, BNB Chain and Monad, bridged with Chainlink CCIP.
- **TVL (DefiLlama):** grew from $33M (Apr 9) to a **peak of $214.1M on Jun 9, 2026**. Saturn itself claims a peak of about $248M. TVL fell after the June STRC crash and the Aug 19 backing migration to about $138M in mid-September, and was **$146.9M on Sep 28, 2026** (+4.2% over 7 days).
- **Composition is rotating:** sUSDat supply keeps shrinking (NAV $66M), while USDat has been growing again since the 3% APR. Monad now carries more circulating USDat than Ethereum.

### Inferences
- Growth was very fast from April to early June (about 6.5x in two months), driven by points and the STRC carry. Three events then reversed it:

## Q6. Community sentiment and controversy on X, Discord, Telegram and Reddit

### Takeaway
Attention rose sharply after the Sep 25 $STRN/TGE thread (about 144k views, well above typical engagement for a 12.9k-follower account), and CT/KOL content is promotional ("points playbooks", YT strategies). Underlying traction had been fading from June to early September (TVL down about 35% from peak). Controversies:
- sUSDat being marketed as a "stablecoin" and then losing about 15% of NAV in June;
