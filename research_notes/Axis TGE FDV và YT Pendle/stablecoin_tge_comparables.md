## Q1. Master comparison: TGE date, TVL at TGE, points→TGE lag, launch / peak / current FDV, drawdown, FDV/TVL

### Takeaway
Across the ~20 stablecoin/yield-dollar tokens that did a TGE between April 2024 and June 2026, almost every 2024–2025 launch is now down 80–99.8% from its all-time high (median drawdown from ATH ≈ -95% for 2025 launches), and launch FDV/TVL multiples fell from ~5x (Ethena, Plasma, Falcon at the open) to ~1x or less (Resolv, Stable, Cap, Re). The 2026 group (Unitas, USD.AI, Cap, Re) launched at much smaller FDVs ($59M–$320M at listing or sale) and is the only group where holders are still above the listing/sale price.

## Q2. Airdrop economics: % of supply, points totals, $/point, and realized "points APY" per $ of TVL

### Takeaway
Airdrop allocations for points seasons cluster at 2.5–10% of supply (Ethena 5% per season, Usual 7.5%, Resolv 10%, Reservoir S1 10%, Falcon 2.5% public at TGE, Stable 10% genesis, Apyx 4–6%, Saturn ≤5%). The realized value per $1 of TVL collapsed from triple-digit annualized rates in Ethena S1 (2024) to single or low-double digits for 2025 launches at realistic sell prices, and to about zero for Level and for later-season holders of collapsed tokens.

- The direction is unambiguous. Launch FDVs dropped by roughly an order of magnitude from the 2024/late-2025 peaks to 2026, and launch FDV/TVL dropped from ~5x to ~1x. The late-2025 cluster (Plasma, Falcon, Stable) was the last high-FDV wave, and it led to some of the steepest drawdowns.
- For a points farmer or YT buyer valuing a new yield-dollar project TGE in Q4 2026, defensible base-case assumptions from these comparables are: launch FDV ≈ 0.5–1.5x TVL at TGE; an airdrop of 3–10% of supply; a realistic sell price 30–70% below the day-1 high; and 50%+ TVL outflow within months. On those inputs the realized points APY is likely in the ~3–15% annualized range on TVL, well below what YT implied APYs usually price in. Delays past YT maturity (Strata, Apyx) are a further downside.
- One caveat to the bearish read: the 2026 low-FDV launches (UP +354%, CAP +73%, CHIP +48% vs sale) show that a low opening FDV can produce positive post-TGE returns. The "cooling" appears in valuation levels more than in the existence of buyers.
