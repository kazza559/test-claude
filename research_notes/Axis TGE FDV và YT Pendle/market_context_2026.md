- Q3 2026 is a bear-market relief rally, not a new uptrend. BTC is +42% from its July low but still about 33% below the peak. ## 2. Stablecoin sector: supply trend, yield-bearing stablecoins, funding/basis yields, regulation, incidents

### Takeaway
Total stablecoin supply has **stagnated at about $305–318B since Nov 2025**. It is about +10% YoY, compared with about +38% in the first 8 months of 2025, and Q2 2026 had the first quarterly decline since 2023. Synthetic and yield-bearing dollars have shrunk sharply. USDe is down about 67% from its peak, USDf down about 44%, and several 2025–26 cohort coins fell 60–100% from peak. sUSDe pays only about 5% while the Fed is at 3.75–4.00%. Three major incidents (the Oct-2025 USDe depeg on Binance, the Nov-2025 Stream/Elixir collapse and the Mar-2026 Resolv USR exploit) damaged trust in the category. On regulation, US law (GENIUS, the proposed CLARITY) targets passive yield on *payment* stablecoins. The EU (BaFin/MiCA) effectively excluded USDe.

**Regulation:**
- **GENIUS Act (enacted July 2025):** bans issuers of *payment* stablecoins from paying interest or yield. The OCC's proposed rule of 2026-02-25 adds a *rebuttable presumption* against indirect yield routed through affiliates or "related third parties" — [Spark research](https://www.spark.money/research/clarity-act-stablecoin-yield-rules); [SSRN, Krause, Mar 2026](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/6458799.pdf?abstractid=6458799&mirid=1). ### Inferences
- The stablecoin *market* is flat, not growing. Growth now goes mainly to regulated or fiat-backed newcomers (USD1, RLUSD, U, USDG) and treasury wrappers, not to crypto-native synthetic dollars. That weakens the usual "stablecoin TAM is exploding" pitch for a yield-dollar token in late 2026.
- sUSDe at about 5% with the Fed at 3.75–4.00% leaves roughly 1–1.5 pp of risk premium. A new yield dollar like Axis must therefore rely on points or incentives rather than organic yield to attract TVL, and must expect that TVL to leave after TGE (see the Cap, USD.AI and Solstice patterns above).
- Three high-profile failures in 11 months (Oct-2025, Nov-2025, Mar-2026) have made curators (Morpho/Gauntlet-type vaults) and exchanges more conservative about listing new yield dollars as collateral. That limits DeFi integrations and the "money-lego" premium in valuation.
- For US-facing distribution, the GENIUS/CLARITY direction (no passive holding yield on payment stablecoins; yield-bearing tokens that try to reclassify as commodities pushed toward securities treatment) creates legal overhang. A new yield dollar will probably geo-block the US and EU, which narrows its exchange and listing options.

### Gaps
- No primary Ethena dashboard data for USDe/sUSDe yield history or BTC/ETH perp funding-rate averages for Q3 2026. The DefiLlama sUSDe APY (about 5%) is the only primary data point collected.
- GENIUS effective date: statute text says the earlier of 18 months after enactment or 120 days after final regulations. This was not re-verified this session, and whether final rules had been adopted by Sep 2026 is unconfirmed.
- CLARITY Act floor status (full Senate or House passage) after May 2026 is not verified.
- No verified 2026-specific MiCA enforcement actions against synthetic dollars other than the 2025 BaFin/Ethena case.

## 3. New token launch (TGE) environment 2025–2026: launch FDVs, post-listing performance, Binance formats, airdrop trends, delays

### Takeaway
Launch conditions are harsh. About 85% of 2025 TGEs trade below their launch valuation, and about 93% of 2024–26 launches that reached a $100M+ market cap are below their launch price, with a median return of about -96%. The 2025 stablecoin/yield cohort was hit hardest: FF -80% since launch, XPL -94% from ATH, USUAL -99% and RESOLV -96% from ATH. The 2026 yield/stablecoin TGEs launched at much lower FDVs, a median of about $300M (range about $80M–650M). Only the smallest-FDV launches (Unitas at about $80M, Cap at about $250M) now trade above their first-day price. Many projects are pushing TGEs into Q4 2026, which creates a crowded calendar.

### Cited Findings

## 4. Points-farming economics via Pendle YT (profitability, implied vs realized $/point, points→TGE time, slippage)

### Takeaway
The environment for points trading on YT has deteriorated sharply. Pendle TVL fell about 91% from its Sep-2025 peak ($13.4B) to about $1.26B now. The 2025 yield-dollar airdrops (Falcon, Resolv, Usual, Plasma) lost 80–99% of token value after TGE, so YTs bought on pre-TGE FDV expectations were likely unprofitable unless bought very early. I found **no rigorous published retrospective** comparing Pendle-implied $/point with realized $/point for 2025–26 projects; this is a key gap. Using stablecoin launch date as a proxy for points start, the time from points launch to TGE for yield dollars runs about 5–12 months (median about 7 months for completed cases), with frequent season extensions and at least one airdrop cut.

### Cited Findings

A secondary source cites $3.44B in Jan 2026 (-74.2% from $13.38B) and PENDLE falling from $6.85 (Aug 2025) to $2.13 — [eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15253995-what-is-pendle-pt-and-yt-tokens-explained-2026)

### Inferences
- **Implied vs realized:** because 2025 cohort tokens fell 80–99% after TGE and airdrops can be cut (Cap -65%), a YT buyer pricing points off pre-TGE "consensus FDV" in 2025–26 would usually have overpaid. Realized $/point likely came in well below the Pendle-implied $/point for most 2025 yield-dollar campaigns. Exceptions are campaigns bought very early at low implied values, or low-FDV launches like Cap and Unitas. This is inferred from token outcomes, not measured directly.
- **Dilution from delays:** each extra month before TGE adds points supply while YT decays. With a median of about 7 months, a range up to about 12+, and visible quarter-long slips, a YT expiring before the actual TGE or snapshot carries timing risk. That risk falls hardest on YTs bought late in a season.
- **Liquidity:** with Pendle TVL down about 91%, YT markets are thinner. Exits are costlier (slippage), and implied yields can swing more on small flows.
- **Bearish framing for a pre-TGE yield dollar in late 2026:** YT points buyers should haircut the implied $/point for (a) launch-FDV compression to the $100M–600M band seen in 2026, (b) post-TGE token drawdown of about 30–80% within weeks to months, (c) airdrop-size cuts, and (d) TGE-date slippage of about one quarter.
