# Perp DEX tokens with TGE between 1 Jul 2024 and 30 Jun 2025: FDV at TGE vs. maximum FDV reached after TGE (cohort 2024H2–2025H1)

## Which perp DEX tokens had their TGE (first public spot trading) in the window, and which candidates were excluded?

### Takeaway
I confirmed 14 main perp-DEX tokens with TGEs in the window: ORDER, LOGX, ADX, MKL, STRIKE, STORM, HYPE, F, BLUE, SYMM, DRV (borderline), KILO, FAF and MYX. Discovery turned up 6 more illiquid micro-caps (CYB, FDX, CYDX, NRS, ZEUZ, SRG) and 4 borderline perp-adjacent tokens (PEAR, TANGO, GS, TYPUS). Three seed-list items fall outside the window: VOOI's TGE was on 18 Dec 2025, and Zeta's ZEX (27 Jun 2024) and Drift's DRIFT (16 May 2024) launched just before 1 Jul 2024. I found no token for ELFi, Moonlander, Perennial or IntentX.

### Cited Findings

#### Method and conventions (apply to every question)
- **Discovery.** I pulled all coins in CoinGecko's "Perpetuals" and "Derivatives" categories (170 coins). I dated each by DefiLlama's first recorded price, which is close to the CoinGecko listing date — [CoinGecko markets, Perpetuals](https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/markets?vs_currency=usd&category=decentralized-perpetuals&per_page=250&page=1&order=market_cap_desc), [CoinGecko markets, Derivatives](https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/markets?vs_currency=usd&category=decentralized-derivatives&per_page=250&page=1&order=market_cap_desc), [DefiLlama first price, example HYPE](https://coins.llama.fi/prices/first/coingecko:hyperliquid). I cross-checked against DefiLlama protocols in the Derivatives and Options categories that have a gecko_id — [DefiLlama protocols](https://api.llama.fi/protocols).
- **Current-value snapshot.** "Current" means the CoinGecko snapshot of 27 Oct 2026. Note that CoinGecko's FDV uses **total** supply: HYPE's FDV of $75.25B = $78.75 × 955.3M total supply, not the 1B max — [CoinGecko HYPE](https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/hyperliquid?localization=false&tickers=false&community_data=false&developer_data=false).
- **Listing-candle artifacts.** On every CEX in this cohort, the opening daily candle opens at a placeholder price and shows a listing wick. Examples: ORDER opens at $0.02 on Gate, KuCoin and Bitget; F at $0.01–0.06; MYX at $0.006 on Bitget; KILO at $0.015 on Gate. Sources: [Gate ORDER daily](https://api.gateio.ws/api/v4/spot/candlesticks?currency_pair=ORDER_USDT&interval=1d&from=1717200000&to=1802736000), [Bitget MYX hourly](https://api.bitget.com/api/v2/spot/market/history-candles?symbol=MYXUSDT&granularity=1h&endTime=1746619200000&limit=200). Because of this, the "opening/first-trade" basis uses the **close of the first trading hour** where hourly data exists (ORDER, F, BLUE, MYX). Otherwise it uses the first DefiLlama/CoinGecko price.
- **Day-1 close and the stricter test.** "Day-1 close" is the UTC daily close on the main CEX. For DEX-only tokens I use DefiLlama's price about 24h after its first price as a proxy. The stricter "pushed higher later" test is the maximum **daily close** after day 7 divided by the day-1 close.

### Inferences
- The window gives about 19 core perp-DEX tokens (including SRG) plus 5 borderline ones. The seed list covered the larger names. Discovery mostly added illiquid DEX-only micro-caps, which matter for survivorship bias because nearly all of them collapsed.
- For several DEX-first tokens (MKL, STRIKE, GS, PEAR, TANGO, TYPUS) the "TGE price" is the first aggregator-tracked price. That price can lag the real TGE by days or weeks, so their multiples carry extra error.

### Gaps
- Exact first-trade timestamps for the DEX-only tokens.
- Whether any small TGE was missed. Tokens outside CoinGecko's categories, or never listed on CoinGecko, would not appear. CryptoRank's API and pages returned 403 and could not be used for cross-checks.

### Takeaway
Measured by intraday all-time highs, every token "exceeded" its TGE FDV at some point. In most cases the gain was modest and temporary. Only HYPE (about 15.7x day-1-close FDV, peak in Sep 2026), MYX (about 207x, Sep 2025), STRIKE (about 28x), FAF (about 6.7x), MKL (about 4x) and CYB (about 4.5x) reached 4x or more. By Oct 2026, 16 of the 18 core tokens trade below their day-1-close FDV; only HYPE and STRIKE are above it.

## Cohort statistics: how often did post-TGE FDV exceed TGE FDV, and does a lower TGE FDV predict a higher peak multiple?

### Takeaway
Measured by intraday ATH, 18/18 core tokens exceeded their TGE FDV, but this is nearly tautological because listing-day wicks count. On the stricter test (a daily close after day 7 at least 1.2x the day-1 close):
- 13 of 18 core tokens (72%) and 11 of 14 main tokens passed.
- Only 9 of 18 core tokens (50%) ever closed at 2x or more, and 4 of 18 at 5x or more.
- 6 of 19 (including SRG) were straight-down listing spikes.
- 16 of 18 trade below TGE FDV today.

A lower TGE FDV is only weakly associated with a higher peak multiple: Spearman ρ is between −0.15 and −0.28, and outliers dominate.

### Inferences
- **On the hypothesis** that low-FDV perp-DEX launches mostly get pushed to a higher FDV later: it holds only in a weak form for this cohort. About three-quarters of tokens, whether low or high FDV, traded at least 1.2x above the day-1-close FDV at some point after week 1. Only about half ever doubled.
  - Low-FDV launches did slightly better at the ≥2x threshold (≈55% vs ≈45%). The rank correlation is weakly negative, which points the same way as the hypothesis, but n is small and the result depends on outliers.
  - The two largest multiples argue against a simple "low FDV → big upside" rule. HYPE had the highest TGE FDV. MYX was mid-range, and its later peak is under manipulation and sybil allegations.
- The micro-cap bucket's high median multiple (~5x) comes from illiquid, DEX-only tokens tracked from an arbitrary first price, many now worth about $0 (CYB, FAF, ZEUZ, CYDX). Their "peak FDV" was mostly unrealisable at size (inference from tiny current liquidity and CoinGecko market lists).
- "Peak after TGE" is a weak investment signal. Nearly every token that peaked later also round-tripped below its TGE FDV, and several protocols wound down or went into maintenance (Adrena, Merkle, Flash Trade, KiloEx after its exploit).

### Gaps
- Small n (18–23) and data-quality issues (first-tracked-price bases, illiquid prints) limit statistical confidence.
- No volume- or liquidity-weighted version of the peaks was computed.
- FDV at the peak does not account for supply changes, for example HYPE burns or FAF inflation to 1.2B.
