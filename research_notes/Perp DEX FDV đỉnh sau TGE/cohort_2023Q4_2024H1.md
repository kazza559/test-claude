## Q1. Which perp DEX tokens had their TGE in the window (Oct 1, 2023 – Jun 30, 2024)?

### Takeaway
Discovery used DefiLlama's Derivatives protocols that carry CoinGecko IDs, the CoinGecko "Perpetuals" and "Derivatives" categories, and seed-list searches, filtered by first recorded trade date. It found 19 perp-DEX tokens (core or borderline) with a TGE in the window, plus 3 small tokens whose TGE date is uncertain. Several seed-list names fall outside the window: RabbitX and Vela launched before it; LogX, Pear, Storm, Merkle, Contango, Flash, Orderly and Hyperliquid launched after it. Satori, Polynomial and Elfi have no token on CoinGecko.

### Inferences
- The CoinGecko/DefiLlama-based discovery is biased toward tokens that are still, or were long, tracked by CoinGecko. Very small tokens that were never listed may be missing, but the dead ones that were listed are included (XEN, PIKO, KAI, TLX, PINGU).
- The cohort is weighted toward the Apr–Jun 2024 period: 10 of the 19 confirmed TGEs happened after BTC's March 2024 top.

### Gaps
- Satori, Polynomial and Elfi: no evidence of a token TGE in the window was found. Whether a token was ever launched off-CoinGecko is unverified.
- The TGE dates for FOX, MST, EMDX and JAV rest only on the first CoinGecko record. No project announcement was checked.
- OX.FUN is widely described as the rebrand of the OPNX exchange token, but no source was retrieved in this pass. It stays excluded and unverified.

### Inferences
- **Timestamp anomaly:** for JUP, LVN, AARK and ZEX, CoinGecko dates the ATH several hours before DefiLlama's first record (JUP: 07:02 UTC, against claims opening at 15:00 UTC). These ATHs are probably thin pre-open or initial-pool prints. Treat TGE-day intraday ATHs as an upper bound, not a tradable peak.
- **Daily data understates peaks:** for coins CoinGecko no longer serves (VRTX, EQU, TLX, PINGU, KAI, XEN, PIKO), "peak" is a daily snapshot and understates intraday highs. Multiples are therefore conservative.
- **Low-float FDVs are notional:** EQU's $538M peak FDV is on max supply while only mined tokens circulated, and HOLD's 30M supply had a small float. These FDV peaks overstate the money that was actually at risk.

### Gaps
- **Pre-market prices** were not found for AEVO, DRIFT or ZEX (Decrypt only says ZEX debuted "slightly above pre-market"). PRCL's figures are aggregated quotes.
- **Private-round valuations** were not found for Vertex, Drift, Zeta, Parcl, Aevo/Ribbon or ZKX (ZKX's $7.6M is total raised, not a valuation).
- **Initial circulating supply** is missing for VRTX, DRIFT, PRCL, ZEX and VELAR.
- **Day-1 venue lists** are only partly sourced. The confirmed ones are JUP (Binance), AEVO (Binance), PRCL (OKX, Bybit), HOLD (Bitget, SyncSwap), ZKX (KuCoin, Gate, Bitget), AARK (Gate) and INTX (Bybit).

- **Opening price cross-checks:** CoinMarketCap was only checked for HOLD (ATH $7.57 vs CoinGecko $7.49). Opening prices for the small caps come from the first CoinGecko record and may be off.
- **Catalysts** are missing for the HOLD (Mar 2024), EQU (Nov–Dec 2023), TLX (May 2024) and XEN (Nov 2023) run-ups.

## Q3. Cohort statistics: were low-FDV TGEs "pushed to a higher FDV" after TGE?

### Takeaway
Most tokens briefly traded above their day-1 FDV. But a meaningful push after the listing week (a daily close at least 1.2x D1 after day 7) happened for only about half: 9 of 17. Big pushes were rare: at least 2x in 7 of 17 and at least 5x in 3 of 17. Lower TGE FDV correlated only weakly with bigger multiples (Spearman ρ ≈ −0.2, n=12). Launch timing within the cycle looks more decisive; see Q4.

### Inferences
- **Strict reading of the hypothesis:** "Low-FDV perp DEX tokens mostly get pushed to a higher FDV at some point" holds only in the trivial intraday sense (100%). On a daily-close basis after the listing week it is a coin flip (9 of 17 reached 1.2x D1 or more), and pushes of 2x or more are a minority (7 of 17).
- **Direction of the FDV effect:** the data mildly supports the idea. Lower-FDV launches have higher median multiples, and every 5x-plus case launched below about $280M FDV. But the effect is weak, the sample is small (n=12 with supply), and low FDV did not prevent straight-down outcomes (AARK at $69M, KAI and FOX).
- **Survivorship:** the dead or delisted small caps (XEN, PIKO, PINGU, TLX, KAI) often had short pumps of 1.4–4x within 2–6 weeks, then went to roughly zero. A "peak FDV" test therefore flatters them relative to any holding outcome.

### Gaps
- n is small and the supply basis is missing for 5 small tokens. With n=12, the FDV correlation is not statistically meaningful.
- Day-1 close is approximated by the CoinGecko price nearest 00:00 UTC as recorded by DefiLlama. For TGEs late in the UTC day (VRTX's first print at 23:49 UTC, JUP at 15:00 UTC) it reflects only hours of trading.
- ZKX and INTX could not be scored on (b)–(d) because no price series was available.
