## 1. Venture funding, M&A and IPOs/SPACs: how much private capital went into stablecoin and stablecoin-payment companies in 2026 vs 2025?

### Takeaway
Private capital is still entering stablecoin infrastructure in 2026. Most of it goes to (a) a small number of late-stage *equity* rounds in payments, cards and neobanking (Rain, KAST, Augustus, Fasset) and (b) *strategic M&A* by payments incumbents (Mastercard–BVNK up to $1.8B; Payward/Kraken–Reap up to $600M). Total crypto VC halved from Q4 2025 to Q1 2026 (~$4.0B) and partly recovered in Q2 ($5.68B). Payments/stablecoins is the #1 category by deal count, but most of its 2026 dollars came through acquisitions. Public-market exits have de-rated: Circle is +8% YTD but −67% from its June 2025 peak, BitGo is −68% below its January 2026 IPO price, and the Open USD consortium coin (June 2026) showed that issuer economics can be competed away.

### Inferences
- **Equity VC into stablecoin companies is roughly flat vs 2025, and M&A is clearly up.** Named 2026 equity rounds sum to ≈$0.75B (Rain, KAST, Fasset ×2, Augustus, Trace, Felix Pago), against ~$1.5B for all of 2025 (SVB), which was itself inflated by Tempo's $500M. Strategic M&A (BVNK + Reap ≈ $2.4B max) already exceeds 2025's Bridge deal ($1.1B). My sum; coverage is incomplete.
- **The share of total crypto VC depends heavily on the tracker:**
  - ~18.5% of Q3 capital (CryptoRank: $685M / $3.7B).
  - ~21% of H1 under Tiger's M&A-inclusive scope.
  - Only ~5% of Galaxy's H1 VC total ($9.68B) if Tiger's non-acquisition portion (~16% × $2.85B ≈ $0.46B) is used. My calc.
- **Capital is going to equity, not tokens.** Revenue businesses (KAST's $100M run-rate, Rain's 38x volume) earn unicorn valuations, while token-based stablecoin projects (Section 4) de-rate. Incumbents (Mastercard, Kraken, Visa/Stripe via Open USD) are buying or building rails directly.
- **Public equity is a weak exit signal.** CRCL is −67% from its peak, and Open USD's yield-sharing model hit its multiple; BTGO is −68% and GEMI −68% YTD.

## 2. Public token sales and pre-deposits: is demand for stablecoin-related sales and vaults rising or falling through 2026?

### Takeaway
Retail token-sale demand **collapsed** in 2026.
- **Trend:** Q1 2025 was the record (~$849M, 429 sales). Q1 2026 fell to ~$389M (105 sales), and Q2 2026 to $40–58M (47 sales, a four-year low). July showed a partial rebound ($121M).
- **Where demand survives:** high oversubscription now appears only in tiny, capped raises (MetaDAO: 127x on a $250K cap).
- **Pre-deposit model:** the 2025 stablechain pre-deposit model (Plasma's $1B vault filled in ~30 minutes; Stable's $825M) has been followed by massive post-TGE outflows. Plasma chain TVL is −90% from its peak and Stable's chain TVL is ~$30M. I found no comparable 2026 pre-deposit campaign.
- **2026 stablecoin TGEs mostly trade below listing:** median −26% vs the day-1 close, 4 of 5 below. CAP (+131%) is the exception.

## 9. What the flow evidence implies: is money still entering the stablecoin narrative, plateauing, or leaving, and which segment still attracts fresh capital?

### Takeaway
**Split verdict.**
- **Equity and strategic capital is still entering**, steady to rising: payments-infra rounds at unicorn valuations, and incumbent M&A of $2.4B+ in 2026.
- **On-chain dollars have plateaued:** stablecoin supply is flat YTD and DeFi stablecoin yields equal T-bills.
- **Token, retail, points and attention capital is leaving:**
  - Public sales are −85–95% from their peak.
  - Pendle is −70% and Plasma −90%.
  - The token basket is −11% YTD and −9% QTD.
  - Social and search attention is down 50–75% from the March 2026 peak.
- **Fresh capital is still going to:** (1) stablecoin payment, card, neobank and FX companies (equity); (2) curated lending and credit (Morpho, Spark, risk curators, Maple); (3) new issuer models that share yield or are distribution-led (USDG, RLUSD, USD1, U, Open USD); (4) a small set of tokens with real cash-flow capture or credible supply management (SKY, MORPHO, ENA's Q3 rebound, CAP).

### Inferences
- **The stablecoin narrative has moved from "token trade" to "equity/infra trade".** Incumbents are buying the rails (Mastercard, Kraken) or co-issuing (Open USD's 140+ backers). VCs fund revenue businesses (cards, remittance, neobanks). Token holders mostly absorbed dilution: points-era TVL left after TGE, and the yield edge disappeared as policy rates rose.
- **Within crypto-native flows,** the only segment showing *fresh, growing* capital is **curated stablecoin credit and lending**:
  - Morpho stablecoin borrows doubled; risk-curator TVL +93%; SparkLend +57%; Maple TVL +9%.

- **Overall: plateauing at the base layer and leaving the speculative layer.** A flow-driven re-rating of stablecoin tokens would need some combination of renewed stablecoin supply growth, a widening DeFi-vs-T-bill spread, a successful large public sale or pre-deposit, or a token event at Tempo, Arc or MetaMask. Polymarket prices all three token launches at ≤8.5% for 2026. My inference.
