## Q1. Which stablecoin-sector projects TGE'd in the last ~12 months, what FDV and FDV/TVL did they get, and is there a downward trend?

### Takeaway
About nine stablecoin-sector tokens launched between Sep 2025 and Sep 2026, and they came in two waves. In late 2025 there were very large "stablecoin chain" launches: Plasma at about $9–10B FDV and Stable at about $2.7B FDV, both at roughly 1.6–5x FDV/TVL. In 2026, yield-dollar protocols launched at much smaller valuations, with day-0 FDVs of $80–650M (median about $300M) and a median FDV/TVL at TGE of about **1.1x** (range 0.7–2.5x). Headline FDVs clearly fell from the late-2025 peak. For yield-dollar protocols, FDV/TVL at TGE stayed roughly flat at 0.7–2.5x, while TVL fell after TGE in 3 of 5 cases.

### Inferences
- FDV fell sharply by wave. Late-2025 "stablecoin chain" launches (XPL about $9.5B, STABLE $2.7B, STBL peak about $6B) were priced like L1s at 1.6–5x on deposits, and all fell 60–90% within 1–3 months. STABLE is the exception: it recovered to its d0 FDV by 2026-09. In 2026, yield-dollar protocols (UP, CHIP, SLX, RE, CAP) launched at $80–650M d0 FDV.
- For yield-dollar protocols, FDV/TVL at TGE did not trend clearly lower over 2026: Mar 1.0x, Apr 2.25x, May 0.74x, Jun 2.5x and 1.1x. The more useful observation is that the market re-rated after TGE based on post-TGE TVL retention and revenue. CAP and RE held or grew TVL and now trade at 1.2–1.9x protocol TVL. SLX lost 46% of its TVL and fell to 0.31x. UP is the outlier at 5.8x despite a 43% TVL decline.
- The closest structural comps for a basis-trading synthetic dollar like Tori (DefiLlama category "Basis Trading", trUSD $73M on 2026-09-27) are Solstice (0.74x → 0.31x) and Unitas (1.0x → 5.8x). The range is very wide, so the choice of anchor matters a great deal.
- Sale and auction prices set a floor reference. In 2026 every token with a public sale traded above its sale FDV on day 0 (UP 16x, CAP 2.3x, CHIP 2.1x, SLX 2.3x). By 2026-09-28, SLX ($66M) was below its $130M Legion sale price, and CHIP ($443M) was above its $300M ICO.

### Gaps
- Exact TGE-day FDV for River, STBL and Plasma: CoinGecko's 365-day window starts 2025-09-29, so these rely on secondary sources.
- Points-program length and airdrop share to points holders for Unitas, Cap (CAP tokens to caps holders, as opposed to the cUSD Stabledrop), and Re: no primary tokenomics page was found or fetched.
- The Cap and Solstice listing venues on day 1 come partly from low-quality aggregators. Official exchange announcements were not fetched.
- No token or TGE was found in the window for Noble, Midas, Perena, Noon, YieldFi, Hyperliquid-ecosystem stablecoins (USDH, Felix) or BTC-backed dollars (Satoshi Protocol, Bitlayer). Searches returned no token data.
- The Stable pre-deposit figures are internally inconsistent in the Bitget piece: Phase 2 is described as "~$1.8B deposits" but the total as "$1.325B".

## Q2. What is the median/mean FDV at TGE and after 1–3 months for this cohort?

### Takeaway
For the 2026 yield-dollar cohort (UP, CHIP, SLX, RE, CAP), the median d0 FDV was about **$298M** (mean $381M). At +30 days the median was about **$230M** (mean $317M), with a median +30d/d0 ratio of **0.79x**. At +90 days the median ratio was **0.64x**, and today it is **0.71x** (median FDV now $443M). The distribution is bimodal. Three tokens lost 30–78% (CHIP, RE, SLX) and two re-rated upward 2–3x from d0 (CAP, UP). Including the late-2025 chain launches makes the picture much worse: XPL was at 0.39x after 30 days and 0.11x now.

### Inferences
- A reasonable base case for a small or mid-size 2026 yield-dollar TGE is a d0 FDV near the median (about $250–300M), a 15–25% decline within 30 days, and then either sustained decline (if TVL leaves) or re-rating (if TVL is retained). TVL retention was the best single discriminator in this small sample: CAP and RE kept TVL and held value, while SLX lost TVL and fell 78%.
- The sample is very small (n=5 for 2026) and survivorship-biased. Neutrl, Apyx, Strata and Level never reached a successful TGE, and their points holders got nothing or are still waiting. Including those "zero" outcomes would lower the expected value of a points program considerably.

### Gaps
- Intraday opening FDVs are available only anecdotally (SLX about $230M open; CAP $0.01 → $0.043 intraday). CoinGecko daily closes understate day-1 volatility.
- No median for the full-market 2026 TGE cohort at 30 and 90 days specifically. The CryptoRank and Memento stats in Q4 cover 2024–2026 and 2025.

## Q3. What were realized airdrop values per $ of TVL-day, and did Pendle YT buyers profit?

### Takeaway
At TGE prices, realized airdrops were worth roughly **3–11% APR on deposited capital** on top of base yield: Solstice about 8–11%, USD.AI about 3–6%, Cap's cUSD Stabledrop about 3.4% on the reduced size. Those values fell 25–80% if tokens were held for 90 days. The clearest documented Pendle YT outcome is Cap. Its Stabledrop was cut from $12M to $4.2M and restricted to YT-only holders who had losses; these were "made whole, but none turned a profit". Solstice YT-USX buyers report large wins, but only in anecdotes. Neutrl YT/points holders appear to face a zero token outcome plus a 49% hit to principal.

### Cited Findings
- Computation method: airdrop USD at TGE price ÷ (average DefiLlama protocol TVL × days in the points season), annualized. ### Inferences
- A 2026 stablecoin airdrop typically delivered about 3–11% annualized on underlying TVL at TGE prices. - Airdrop value per $-day was higher where TVL was smaller relative to FDV (Solstice) and lower where large ICOs took most of the user allocation (USD.AI: 70% to ICO, 30% to airdrop).

### Inferences
- The points/YT meta that drove 2025 (Ethena seasons, $13B Pendle TVL, $1–10B FDV launches) is largely over. Farm capital is about an order of magnitude smaller ($1.26B Pendle TVL), which also means less competition for each point. That is a double-edged fact for a new points program: less dilution, but also fewer marginal YT buyers to push implied APYs up.
- Base yields for delta-neutral/basis strategies have compressed (sUSDe about 4–5%). Protocols advertising much higher yield are either using leverage or concentration (Unitas per Pine Analytics) or taking credit, RWA or strategy risk (Neutrl, Apyx/STRC). The market has now seen these risks realized, so risk premia on new synthetic dollars should be higher than in 2025.
- Regulation benefits the regulated payment-stablecoin incumbents and treasury-backed tokens more than crypto-native yield dollars. GENIUS does not directly ban synthetic-dollar yield, but the relative momentum has shifted toward T-bill products (USDY and USYC growing while sUSDe and sUSDS shrink).
- Net: the stablecoin-sector narrative is "cooled but stabilizing" as of late Sep 2026. A TGE in Q4 2026 would face lower FDV/TVL expectations than in 2025, but a less crowded field and a modestly improving tape.
