## 5. Community sentiment and concerns: point inflation, whale concentration, Exponent YT farming, loopers, APY changes, incidents

### Takeaway
Community sentiment is broadly positive or neutral: OnRe is seen as a "steady" farm with the most consistent yield on Solana. The measurable concerns are (a) **point inflation**, with total points up about 4.4x since March 2026 and daily issuance about 2.5x faster than in spring, and (b) **heavy concentration**, where the top 10 wallets hold about 27% and the top 100 about 52% of all points. Frequent 10x–16x boost campaigns favor looped and YT capital. APY is edging down (about 11.0% in late September). I found no reported exploits or depegs.

### Cited Findings
- **Official leaderboard snapshot (my pull of all 24 pages from the rewards API, 2026-09-29):** 23,899 wallets; **240.9B total points**; top 1 wallet 8.1%; **top 10 27.4%**; **top 100 52.3%**; top 1,000 84.7%; median wallet about 72,095 points. Points attributed to the `exponent.yt` field are 16.3% of all points. The largest wallet (19.5B points) earned most of them through Loopscale (13.25B) and Kamino (5.69B). — [OnRe rewards API](https://rewards.api.onre.finance/api/v1/points/leaderboard?page=0&size=1000); [app leaderboard](https://app.onre.finance/earn/leaderboard)

### Inferences
- ONyc supply is roughly 262M (a $300M AUM figure divided by about $1.146), so 1.68B points/day implies an **effective average multiplier of about 6x per ONyc-day**. Boosted loop and YT capital therefore dominates issuance, and plain holders (1x) are heavily diluted.
- Concentration is high: the top 100 wallets hold more than half of all points. Any points-pro-rata airdrop would go mostly to large loopers and institutional-scale wallets unless OnRe applies caps or tiering. I found no public discussion of such caps.
- Sybil concerns are low-salience in public discussion. The 100-ONyc referral activation threshold and the "capital efficiency > spam" framing suggest OnRe designed against low-value sybils, but I found no explicit sybil-filter statement.
- A falling APY together with rising points issuance means the yield-plus-points trade is getting weaker at the margin for new YT buyers.

### Gaps
- I found no forum, Reddit or X threads explicitly complaining about dilution, sybil activity or whale dominance. The concerns above come from my own data analysis, not quoted community complaints.
- The community tracker's wallet counts (about 19.5k–22.9k) differ from the official leaderboard's 23,899. The tracker's method is unclear.
